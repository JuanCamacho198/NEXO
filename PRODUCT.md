# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

NEXO primarily serves habitual readers who want to resume reading immediately across desktop and mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

NEXO provides a continuous reading environment for importing, organizing, reading, and tracking books. Success means a reader can return to the product and continue from the correct place with minimal friction.

## Positioning

NEXO is built around cross-device reading continuity: the reader can resume where they stopped with synchronized reading progress across desktop and mobile.

## Operating Context

Readers maintain a personal library, import books, discover content, read across desktop and Android, and use progress, goals, streaks, statistics, notes, highlights, and a dictionary to support their reading practice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Desktop and Android experiences belong to the same product but adapt their interaction patterns to each platform.
- The product supports book import, library management, discovery, addons, reading progress, goals, streaks, statistics, notes, highlights, and dictionary access.
- The desktop Home surface must prioritize resuming the active reading session.
- Advanced library management remains available through the dedicated Library surface.
- The exact synchronization guarantees and offline conflict behavior remain product decisions to document separately.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: NEXO.
- Existing NEXO logo assets and established product terminology must be preserved unless a separate rebrand is explicitly authorized.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing desktop and Android implementations provide the current functional product evidence.
- Existing repository assets provide the current NEXO identity evidence.
- No testimonials, usage benchmarks, or external product claims are confirmed and they must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Resume reading before presenting administration.
- Preserve continuity across devices and interruptions.
- Keep the reader oriented through visible progress and clear state.
- Reveal library-management complexity only when it is needed.
- Adapt interactions to the platform without fragmenting the product experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core reading, navigation, progress, and library actions must remain understandable and operable with keyboard and assistive technology. Text and interactive states must meet WCAG AA contrast expectations.

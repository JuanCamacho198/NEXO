# NEXO - Design Tokens

## Colors

### Backgrounds
- `bg-main`: `#0B1120` (Dark, almost black blue)
- `bg-card`: `#161F33` (Slightly lighter blue for cards)
- `bg-card-hover`: `#1E293B`

### Typography
- `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF`
- `text-secondary`: `#94A3B8`
- `text-accent`: `#3B82F6` (Blue text like "Ver todo")

### Accents / Brand
- `primary-blue`: `#3B82F6` (Main brand color, progress bars, buttons)
- `primary-blue-hover`: `#2563EB`
- `icon-bg-blue`: `#1E3A8A` (Darker blue for icon backgrounds)

### Status / Categories (Quick Access)
- `accent-yellow`: `#EAB308`
- `bg-yellow-transparent`: `#423419`
- `accent-green`: `#22C55E`
- `bg-green-transparent`: `#143A27`
- `accent-purple`: `#A855F7`
- `bg-purple-transparent`: `#311C4A`
- `accent-blue`: `#3B82F6`
- `bg-blue-transparent`: `#1E3A8A`

## Typography

- **Font Family**: Inter, Roboto, or system default (sans-serif)
- **H1 (Greeting)**: 24px, Bold
- **H2 (Section Titles)**: 18px, Semi-Bold
- **Subtitle**: 14px, Regular
- **Body**: 14px, Regular
- **Small Text**: 12px, Regular

## Spacing & Layout

- **Screen Padding**: 20px (left and right)
- **Section Gap**: 32px
- **Card Padding**: 16px
- **Border Radius**:
  - Cards: 16px
  - Buttons: 8px
  - Avatars/Icons: Full (50%) or 12px for squaricles

## Elevations / Shadows
- Minimal to no shadows due to dark mode. Rely on background color contrast (bg-main vs bg-card) and subtle borders.
